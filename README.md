# Yordan Bozhkov

**Technology · Product · Design**  
*Building software that earns its place in people's lives.*

I work across product strategy, software systems, and experience design—turning ambitious ideas into focused, useful products. I care equally about what a product **does**, how it **feels**, and whether it **works in the real world**.

### What I focus on

- **Product engineering:** shaping useful workflows, reliable software, and clear technical foundations.
- **AI and automation:** practical applications with purposeful human control—not AI for its own sake.
- **Design and experience:** distinctive interfaces, accessible interactions, and high standards of fit and finish.
- **From concept to release:** research, prototyping, implementation, evidence, and product-owner acceptance.

### How I think about building

**Useful over fashionable.** A feature should solve a meaningful problem.  
**Evidence over claims.** Passing tests matters; so do real workflows, good design, and honest limitations.  
**Bold by design.** Different products deserve distinct identities—not the same dashboard wearing another color.  
**Ownership over noise.** Keep the essential experience understandable and under the user's control.
